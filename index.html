Welcome to the Maine Makers Workshop!

Maine Makers Workshop is a community space for people who want to learn practical skills, explore creative ideas, and build projects with their own hands. Our workshops are designed for beginners, hobbyists, students, and experienced makers who want to develop new skills in woodworking, crafts, electronics, 3D printing, and other hands-on activities. Whether you are learning to use tools for the first time or looking for a place to work on your next project, Maine Makers Workshop provides instruction, shared workspace, and a community of people who enjoy making things.
