IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Membership is not equipment clearance.

Membership does not automatically authorize use of every tool or machine. Some equipment requires orientation, training, instructor approval, or separate authorization before use.

General access tools

Common hand tools, measuring tools, selected craft tools, and approved work surfaces may be used after basic orientation or staff instruction.

Training-required equipment

Selected power tools, soldering equipment, 3D printers, and other specialized maker equipment require specific instruction before independent use.

Restricted equipment

Some equipment may require staff supervision, advanced training, age restrictions, or specific authorization.

MEMBERSHIP GUIDELINES

Shared space works when everyone helps.

  • Membership applies only to the registered member.
  • Membership does not provide unrestricted tool access.
  • Members must follow all safety rules.
  • Tool authorization may be suspended for unsafe use.
  • Clean work areas after use and return shared tools to their designated locations.
  • Personal projects should not block shared workspace.
  • Storage is limited and must be approved.
  • Hazardous or inappropriate materials may not be brought into the makerspace without approval.
  • Report damaged equipment; do not attempt unauthorized repairs.

Membership details and guidelines

Day Pass — $20 per day

A Day Pass provides access to approved shared workspace areas during eligible operating hours. It is appropriate for occasional visitors, people working on short projects, members who do not need monthly access, and visitors trying the makerspace before purchasing a membership. A Day Pass does not automatically authorize use of restricted equipment. Some tools require separate safety training.

Monthly Maker — $65 per month

The Monthly Maker membership provides regular access during standard member workshop hours.

  • Shared workspace access
  • Approved basic hand-tool access
  • Member workshop discounts
  • Access to community maker areas
  • Participation in eligible member activities

Restricted equipment may require separate authorization.

Unlimited Maker — $110 per month

The Unlimited Maker membership is intended for frequent users who want expanded makerspace access.

  • Expanded access during approved operating hours
  • Shared workspace
  • Eligible equipment use after required training
  • Workshop discounts
  • Limited project storage
  • Member maker-area access

Unlimited Maker does not mean unrestricted use of every tool. All equipment rules still apply.

Tool access categories

General access tools

Tools that may be used after basic orientation or staff instruction. Examples may include common hand tools, measuring tools, selected craft tools, and approved work surfaces.

Training-required equipment

Equipment that requires specific instruction before independent use. Examples may include selected power tools, soldering equipment, 3D printers, and other specialized maker equipment.

Restricted equipment

Frequently asked questions

Can I use any tool after purchasing a membership?

No. Certain tools and equipment require training, orientation, staff approval, or specific authorization.

Can I bring my own project?

Members may work on approved personal projects in designated areas. Projects must comply with safety requirements, workspace rules, equipment restrictions, and available storage.

Can children use the makerspace?

Some activities may be appropriate for families or younger participants, but age requirements depend on the workshop and equipment involved. The website does not imply unrestricted access for children.
